GEMINI SCREENPLAY STUDIO
========================

Thank you for downloading Gemini Screenplay Studio!
This AI-powered tool helps you brainstorm, outline, and write feature films and TV pilots.

INSTALLATION (First Time Only)
------------------------------
Because this app was not downloaded from the App Store, macOS requires a security override.

1. Unzip the "GeminiScreenplayStudio" file.
2. Open the "Terminal" app on your Mac (Cmd+Space, type "Terminal").
3. Type the following command (but do not hit Enter yet):
   xattr -cr 
   (Notice the space after -cr)
4. Drag and drop the "GeminiScreenplayStudio.app" onto the Terminal window.
5. Hit Enter.

Now you can double-click the app to run it!

USAGE
-----
1. Double-click "GeminiScreenplayStudio".
2. A website will open automatically: http://localhost:5001
3. Click "API Key" to enter your Google Gemini API Key.
4. Click "+ New Project" to start writing!

QUITTING THE APP
----------------
To completely stop the background engine:
31. Scroll to the top right of the dashboard.
32. Click the "Quit" button (or use the button in the footer).
3. Wait for the "System Offline" message, then close the tab.

WHERE ARE MY FILES?
-------------------
All your screenplays are saved safely in:
Documents > GeminiScreenplayStudio > output

Enjoy writing!
